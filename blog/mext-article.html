MEXT Application:
My Journey and Experience back in 2023-24

October 11, 2026·MEXT Scholarship

Hello, World!
Every MEXT Application cycle (which usually begins around April every year), I tend to receive a lot of messages asking me about the application process, or tips and tricks to improve one's chances of getting accepted. While I do try my best to keep up with all the messages I receive, it gets very hard to reply personally to every person. I have also been trying to compile some articles on LinkedIn, it appears so that no one has been reading them that often. This blog is an attempt to consolidate all the articles in one place so that I can just share a single link to everyone reading them. Starting with this first article, I want to talk about my experience as an applicant back in 2023.

My Background and Preparation

I learned about MEXT scholarship probably when I was a second-year Engineering student from Dr. Shreya Santra, a MEXT alumni who finished her doctoral studies at Tohoku University. She had written an article back then talking more about the scholarship, and her path to obtaining it. She is an amazing person, who has been helpful with a lot of questions I asked. Unfortunately the article isn't around anymore, but it is definitely one reason why I chose to write articles of my own. Coming to me now. I would describe myself as academically average, from a not-so-big university, but curious and initiative-driven. If you take a look at my About Me page, you'll know more about Team Avadhi, Ardra, Airhaven, and also about my other projects. As a prospective aerospace engineer, I was interested in studying Astrodynamics, and Guidance, Navigation and Control (GNC), very challenging fields to enter in my opinion.
