Hello, World!

While I do try my best to keep up with all the messages I receive, it gets very hard to reply personally to every person. I have also been trying to compile some articles on LinkedIn, it appears so that no one has been reading them that often. This blog is an attempt to consolidate all the articles in one place so that I can just share a single link to everyone reading them. Starting with this first article, I want to talk about my experience as an applicant back in 2023.
