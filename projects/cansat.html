IN-SPACe National CANSAT Competition

Student spacecraft systems engineering, integration, and field testing carried out from 2022 to 2024.

Project Summary

This project involved the design and integration of a compact CANSAT platform for the IN-SPACe National CANSAT Competition. The work covered subsystem coordination, avionics integration, mechanical packaging, testing, and operational preparation for the national finals held in Ahmedabad in April 2024.
