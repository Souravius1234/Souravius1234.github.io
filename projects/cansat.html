IN-SPACe National CANSAT Competition

Student spacecraft systems engineering, integration, and field testing carried out from 2022 to 2024.

2022–2024CanSatSystems engineeringStudent competition

Overview

This project involved the design and integration of a compact CanSat platform for the IN-SPACe National CANSAT Competition. The work included subsystem coordination, avionics integration, mechanical packaging, testing, and preparation for the national finals in Ahmedabad in April 2024.

This page is a developing technical record of the project. Architecture details, test data, and original photographs will be incorporated as they become available.

Project Objectives

Mission requirements

Define the functional, environmental, mechanical, and operational requirements of the CanSat system.

Subsystem development

Integrate avionics, communications, power, structure, and recovery subsystems into a compact platform.

Verification and testing

Establish a sequence of subsystem checks, integration tests, and mission-readiness assessments.

Field operations

Prepare procedures for deployment, telemetry monitoring, retrieval, and post-test evaluation.
